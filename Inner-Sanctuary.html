Before you publish

Inner Sanctuary is a space for sincere reflection and creative expression. Before sharing with the community, please confirm that your writing:

  • Does not mock, misrepresent, or demean any religion or belief
  • Does not contain hate speech or discriminatory language
  • Does not exploit religious symbols or texts for provocation
  • Is your own original work, or clearly attributed to its source
  • Is written in a spirit of sincerity, care, and good faith

By publishing you agree to these community guidelines.
