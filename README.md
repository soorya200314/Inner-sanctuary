# Inner Sanctuary ✦

A quiet, open-source web space for reflection, wisdom, and community mental health support. 

## Project Vision
Inner Sanctuary is designed to be a digital retreat. It combines ancient wisdom from various traditions with modern mental health resources and creative tools like haiku generation and private journaling.

## Key Features
- **Wisdom Feed**: Curated quotes from Stoic, Vedic, Buddhist, and Secular traditions.
- **Journallig**: Space for people for personal journalling
- **Community feed**: Space for people to use their creative skills for writing poems, haiku and quotes and publishing it in the community space
- **Meditative space**: Breathing exercise dashboards for quick mind relaxation

- To create a space for therapy sessions with our community of healers.
