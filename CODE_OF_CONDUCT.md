# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- **Be Kind**: Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- **Respect Privacy**: Many users use this space for reflection. Respect the sanctity of personal thoughts.
- **Empathy**: This is a mental health-focused project. Always interact with empathy and grace.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project lead (Soorya Narayan). The project lead has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
