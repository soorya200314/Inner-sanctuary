# Contributing to Inner Sanctuary

Thank you for helping us "Heal the World." Because this project involves mental health support, we have high standards for contributions.

### How Can I Help?
1. **Add Wisdom**: Propose new quotes or poems. Please ensure they are attributed to the original author.
2. **Resource Updates**: Suggest new mental health helplines or support groups.
3. **Book Recommendations**: Suggest books for the side panel that focus on philosophy, science, or wellness.

### Submission Guidelines
- **Sincerity First**: All additions must be made in a spirit of care and good faith.
- **No Misinformation**: Scientific or medical advice must be backed by reputable sources.
- **Formatting**: Please maintain the aesthetic of the "Inner Sanctuary" (clean, minimal, and calm).

### The Process
- Fork the repo.
- Create a new branch for your feature.
- Submit a Pull Request (PR) for review. I (Soorya) will review all PRs personally.
